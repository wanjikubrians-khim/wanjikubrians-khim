## 🚀 Featured Project — Mama Fua

> *Formalizing home services in Kenya, one booking at a time.*

**Mama Fua** is a two-sided marketplace platform that connects clients who need cleaning and home services with vetted professional cleaners and mama fuas (domestic workers) in Kenya.

- 🏠 Clients can browse, book, and pay for trusted home service professionals
- 👩‍💼 Workers get access to steady income, ratings, and financial tools
- 🔒 All service providers are vetted for safety and quality
- 📱 Built for the Kenyan market — mobile-first, locally relevant
